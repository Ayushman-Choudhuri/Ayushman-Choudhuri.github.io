About Me

Robotics Engineer  ·  Researcher

I'm Ayushman Choudhuri, a robotics engineer and AI researcher. I completed my M.Sc. at RWTH Aachen University in Germany in 2025, with a focus on computer vision and autonomous systems.

I describe myself as a "Specialized Generalist" — someone who has developed deep expertise in robot perception while maintaining the breadth to collaborate across the full hardware-software stack. The most impactful solutions emerge at the intersection of disciplines.

My research has focused on the frontier of explainable AI and 3D perception — making transformer-based models not just accurate, but interpretable, a critical requirement for deploying AI in safety-critical autonomous systems.
