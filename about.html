About Me

Robotics Software Engineer  ·  Computer Vision

I'm Ayushman Choudhuri, a robotics software engineer specializing in computer vision and autonomous systems. Currently building production CV systems at Schmiede.one GmbH in Düsseldorf, Germany. I hold an M.Sc. in Robotic Systems Engineering from RWTH Aachen University (2025).

I describe myself as a "Specialized Generalist" — deep expertise in robot perception with the breadth to collaborate across the full hardware-software stack, from edge deployment to MLOps pipelines. The most impactful solutions emerge at the intersection of disciplines.

My work spans real-time 3D object detection, explainable AI for transformer models, and end-to-end MLOps — building systems that are not just accurate but production-ready and interpretable, critical for deploying AI in safety-critical environments.
